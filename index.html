Why the name

Why in-Between?

Most of what we eat isn’t part of a meal.

It is the handful of something between meetings. The snack packed for school. Something brought out when friends visit. A box carried home for family. A gift sent because an occasion deserves one.

These small moments sit in between the big ones. That’s the space we’re building for.

The in-Between standard

Premium need not be extravagant. It needs to be thoughtful.
