Why in-Between?

Most of what we eat isn’t part of a meal.
It’s something between meetings. A snack packed for the day. Something brought out when friends drop by. A box carried home for family. A gift sent because an occasion deserves one. These small moments sit in between the big ones. That’s the space we’re building for.

Our Philosophy

Our standard is personal.

Chosen with care. Made to be shared.

Before something becomes part of in-Between, there’s a simple question we come back to:

“Would we be happy to put it on our own table and share it with the people we care about?”

If the answer isn’t yes, it doesn’t make the cut. We like familiar foods done well, combinations that have a reason to exist, and presentation that adds to the experience without overwhelming it.

About

Built around the moments in between.

We’re building deliberately — choosing things we genuinely enjoy and creating gifts we would be happy to give ourselves. For now, that means doing fewer things, and doing them with care.
